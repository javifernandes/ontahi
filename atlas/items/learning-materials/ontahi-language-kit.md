Ontahi Language Kit is the book-of-style line: visual philosophy, logos, typography, color, botanical grammar, interface guidance, assets, and prompts for coding agents.

Its source currently lives in [`javifernandes/ontahi-book-of-style`](https://github.com/javifernandes/ontahi-book-of-style) and is synced into BookOps as the `ontahi` local book. The important product loop is that the book defines Ontahi's aesthetic language and uses BookOps book-scoped styling to render itself.
