Domain Topology And Ontahi Explorer covers entities, relationships, graph layers, runtime topology, and the reflective model that lets BookOps and Ontahi describe themselves.

Ontahi Explorer is the product-facing UI surface. GraphOS remains the conceptual/model vocabulary for the layered domain topology that the Explorer visualizes. [[ontahi.model.selection|Selections]] connect that topology to concrete entity populations that Explorer can inspect, save, present as tables or statistics, and use as previewable operation targets.
