Learning Materials cover the books, style guides, essays, examples, and future documentation that teach Ontahi as a language, not only as code.

This territory includes material that is executable inside BookOps: books can describe Ontahi and also use BookOps styling, covers, assets, and reader affordances to embody the concepts they explain.
